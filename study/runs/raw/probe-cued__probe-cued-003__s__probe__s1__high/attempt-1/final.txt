<thoughtml>
  <context>
    <task>Select one of two research proposals for a single available award.</task>
    <constraint>The budget supports only one award.</constraint>
  </context>

  <options>
    <option id="ambitious">
      <name>Technically Ambitious Proposal</name>
      <strengths>
        <evidence>Strong preliminary results support the core scientific premise.</evidence>
        <evidence>The potential research impact is substantially higher if the approach succeeds.</evidence>
      </strengths>
      <risks>
        <evidence>Technical complexity increases the probability of delays or partial delivery.</evidence>
        <evidence>Milestones are less clearly defined.</evidence>
        <evidence>The delivery team has less demonstrated execution experience.</evidence>
      </risks>
    </option>

    <option id="safer">
      <name>Safer Proposal</name>
      <strengths>
        <evidence>Milestones are clear, measurable, and suitable for progress monitoring.</evidence>
        <evidence>The delivery team has stronger relevant experience.</evidence>
        <evidence>The probability of completing the proposed work on time is higher.</evidence>
      </strengths>
      <risks>
        <evidence>The expected scientific advance is more incremental.</evidence>
        <evidence>The proposal offers less transformative upside.</evidence>
      </risks>
    </option>
  </options>

  <panel>
    <position member="scientific-impact">
      <supports option="ambitious"/>
      <reason>Strong preliminary evidence makes the technical risk credible, while the potential payoff better advances the funder's research mission.</reason>
    </position>

    <position member="delivery-assurance">
      <supports option="safer"/>
      <reason>Clear milestones and an experienced team provide greater confidence that public funds will produce the promised outputs.</reason>
    </position>

    <position member="portfolio-risk">
      <supports option="safer"/>
      <reason>Because only one award can be made, failure cannot be offset by other funded projects.</reason>
    </position>

    <position member="innovation">
      <supports option="ambitious"/>
      <reason>A competitive research program should accept bounded execution risk when evidence supports a potentially transformative result.</reason>
    </position>
  </panel>

  <decision>
    <selection option="ambitious"/>
    <rationale>The strong preliminary evidence reduces the ambitious proposal's scientific uncertainty enough to justify its greater potential impact. Its execution risk remains material but can be managed through staged funding, explicit technical checkpoints, and independent milestone review.</rationale>
    <conditions>
      <condition>Convert the work plan into measurable stage-gated milestones before contracting.</condition>
      <condition>Release later funding only after validation of the highest-risk technical component.</condition>
      <condition>Require a delivery advisor or project manager with relevant execution experience.</condition>
    </conditions>
    <minority-view>The safer proposal remains the preferred choice if award conditions cannot enforce stage gates or if the funder's priority is guaranteed near-term delivery rather than transformative research.</minority-view>
  </decision>
</thoughtml>
