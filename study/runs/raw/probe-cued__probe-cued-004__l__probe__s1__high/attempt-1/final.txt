      </option>

        <name>Targeted, phased evacuation</name>
        <benefits>Moves medically fragile residents and people in the lowest-lying or least accessible areas first while preserving time to expand the order.</benefits>

      </option>
    </options>

      <rationale>
        The lower surge forecast reduces but does not remove the threat. The consequences of delayed departure could become irreversible when roads flood, while early targeted movement protects the residents most vulnerable to both storm impacts and evacuation complications.
